# Business Metrics and Statistical Testing Analysis  
## Comparative Evaluation Between Group A and Group B

## Project Objective
The goal of this study is to evaluate the performance of Groups A and B across key business metrics — conversion rate, number of orders, total revenue, and average ticket — and to statistically validate whether the observed differences are significant.  
The analysis combines:

- Raw performance metrics  
- Outlier treatment  
- Daily stability evaluation  
- Formal statistical hypothesis testing (A/B test)  

---

## Key Findings

### **1. Group B outperforms Group A in all major metrics**
In the raw data, Group B shows superior performance in:

- **Conversion rate**
- **Total number of orders**
- **Total revenue**

These metrics are more stable and better represent the real value generated by the experiment.

---

### **2. Impact of outliers on average ticket**
After removing outliers:

- Group B’s average ticket decreases, as it was influenced by a few extremely high orders  
- However, **the overall conclusion remains unchanged**:  
  Group B still leads in the most important and stable metrics

This confirms that the result is not driven by extreme values.

---

### **3. Daily conversion: natural volatility, consistent advantage**
Daily conversion rates fluctuate naturally, but:

- Both groups follow similar patterns  
- Group B maintains a **slight but consistent advantage** throughout the period  

This indicates stability in the observed effect.

---

### **4. Statistical tests confirm the result**
Statistical hypothesis tests show that:

- The differences between the groups **are not due to chance**  
- Group B performs significantly better  
- The conclusion remains robust even after data cleaning and outlier removal  

---

## Final Conclusion

**Group B is the best option for implementation.**

It:

- **Generates more total value**  
- **Shows higher efficiency**  
- **Maintains consistent performance**  
- **Remains superior even after outlier treatment**

The recommendation is to proceed with implementing Variant B.
